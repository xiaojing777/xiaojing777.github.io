About

I am a fifth-year Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Fei Xue and Prof. Jianxi Su. Prior to Purdue, I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Economics at Xiamen University in 2022, where I was advised by Prof. Jingyuan Liu.

My research centers on uncertainty quantification across tail risk modeling, heterogeneity analysis, and calibrated auto-modeling, with applications to wearable device, insurance, and clinical trial data. More recently, I have also been working on combining statistical thinking with AI. Through internships and collaborations at Novartis, Munich Re, and Eli Lilly, I aim to bring these methods into practice. If you are interested in potential collaboration, please feel free to reach out.
