About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Fei Xue and Prof. Jianxi Su. I also maintain a long-term research collaboration with Prof. Xiao Wang. Prior to Purdue, I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Economics at Xiamen University in 2022, where I was advised by Prof. Jingyuan Liu.

I work on uncertainty quantification, finite-sample guarantees, risk control, and interpretability across complex data settings (e.g., wearable data, tail-risk modeling), and have recently been bringing these ideas to frontier AI research to make AI systems reliable and safe.
